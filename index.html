Our Process
01
Inspection & Assessment

Every vehicle begins with a thorough inspection under high-intensity lighting. We document paint condition, swirl marks, and contamination before a single product touches the surface.

02
Decontamination

A full chemical decontamination removes iron fallout, tar, and industrial pollution. Clay bar treatment follows to lift embedded surface contaminants safely.

03
Paint Correction

Machine polishing by hand removes swirl marks, scratches, and oxidation. We refine the paint to a concours finish — the foundation for any coating or protection.

04
Protection & Coating

Ceramic coating, paint protection film, or premium wax is applied depending on your chosen tier. Each protects your investment and amplifies depth and gloss.
